# What Ekwo is, and what it is not

Ekwo OS is accounting software. It is not accounting, tax or legal advice,
and installing it does not make anyone your accountant.

## The books are yours

You are responsible for the accuracy of your books, your returns and your
filings, in every country where you file. Ekwo helps you keep them; it does
not keep them for you, and it does not know facts that are not in the
database. A figure Ekwo computes — a VAT box, a balance sheet line, a tax
estimate — is only as right as the entries behind it and the rules it was
given.

## What a country pack is

A country pack is our reading of a country's rules — chart of accounts, taxes,
declaration boxes, financial statements — at the date of its version. Rules
change; a pack can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date for your situation.
Each rule cites its legal source so that you, or your accountant, can check
it. The golden test proves that a pack is consistent with itself, not that
it is consistent with the law.

## What the statuses mean

- **community**: contributed by someone; nobody has reviewed it.
- **maintained**: written and kept up to date by Ekwo; not reviewed by an
  accounting professional.
- **reviewed**: read by a named professional, on the date shown, against the
  rules they apply in practice.

A review is a good-faith reading by a professional, given to the community
for free. It is not an engagement letter, an audit, or a guarantee. Neither
the reviewer nor Ekwo assumes liability for what you file on the strength
of it. If you need someone to answer for your filings, engage them.

## Estimates

Some functions produce estimates by design — a tax estimate during the year,
a cash-basis view, a carbon footprint. They say so. An estimate is not a
declaration, and nothing in Ekwo OS files anything with any authority.
